# Optional Features, AI and Admin — Phases 23 to 29

---

## Phase 23 — Calendar
**What this phase does:** Keeps the calendar as **DEFERRED** (not built) until you decide whether it ships (D04). If approved later, it would build events from real learning data.

**What you can expect when finished:** For now, only a clear, truthful treatment of the demo calendar route (April 2024 sample events). A real calendar happens only after your approval.

**Needs:** Phase 22. **Status:** PLANNED, scope DEFERRED.

---

## Phase 24 — Notifications
**What this phase does:** Keeps notifications as **DEFERRED** (D05). If approved, it would add an inbox with read state and respect each learner's saved preferences.

**What you can expect when finished:** For now, the header's notification dot and the preference toggles are not claimed as real delivery.

**Needs:** Phase 22. **Status:** PLANNED, scope DEFERRED.

---

## Phase 25 — AI Learning Assistant
**What this phase does:** Replaces the scripted demo chat with a real OpenAI-backed assistant limited to the learner's enrolled course content. Answers carry citations, with rate and cost limits.

**What you can expect when finished:** An assistant that answers only from content the learner is allowed to see, and says so when it cannot. Chat history is not saved in V1.

**Needs:** Phases 08, 10 and 12. **Open decisions:** D21 (AI scope), D03 (privacy).

---

## Phase 26 — Community and Messages
**What this phase does:** Records that community and messages are **DEFERRED**, and gets a decision on how the placeholder pages and nav links appear at launch.

**What you can expect when finished:** A clear decision on those placeholder routes. No chat, forum or moderation system is built.

**Needs:** Phase 00. **Open decision:** D22.

---

## Phase 27 — Course and Content Administration
**What this phase does:** Builds the admin screens to create, edit, preview, publish and archive courses, modules, lessons, translations, resources, videos, quizzes and learning paths.

**What you can expect when finished:** Staff can manage all content from the website. Today courses are created through database functions, with no admin UI.

**Needs:** Phases 06, 08, 11, 14 and 21. **Open decisions:** D12 (launch content), D16.

---

## Phase 28 — Learner and Enrollment Administration
**What this phase does:** Gives support staff a least-privilege way to look up a learner, grant or revoke access manually, and handle completion and certificate problems. Sensitive reads and changes are audited with a reason.

**What you can expect when finished:** A support console that does not expose more learner data than needed. Organization manager reports are out of scope.

**Needs:** Phases 06 and 18. **Open decision:** D03.

---

## Phase 29 — Commerce Administration
**What this phase does:** Shows orders, payments, reconciliation and failed webhook events to admins, and offers only the approved refund and override actions, with audit.

**What you can expect when finished:** Finance and support can see what happened to any payment and fix failed ones. It is not an accounting suite.

**Needs:** Phases 06 and 20. **Open decision:** D02 (refunds).
